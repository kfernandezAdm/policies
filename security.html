Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-10-17

Who we are

Appliversed is a tech services business that builds and tests software solutions before production use.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, and related profile details you provide.
  • Service data: content and metadata processed by our integrations (e.g., WhatsApp Business Cloud API IDs, message metadata, timestamps).
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser info, cookies, logs, diagnostics.

Why we collect it

  • To provide and support our software and automation services.
  • To test products and features before implementing them in production and to ensure quality, reliability, and compliance.
  • To secure our systems, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Legal bases (where applicable)

  • Performance of a contract.
  • Legitimate interests (service testing, security, improvement).
  • Consent, when required.
  • Compliance with law.

Sharing

We share data with service providers that process data for us (e.g., hosting, analytics, communications such as Meta’s WhatsApp Business Cloud API) under contracts and only for the purposes above. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by law. Test data is minimized and deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

  • Request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and auditing. No method is 100% secure.

Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13.

International transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your own. We use appropriate safeguards where required.

Changes
